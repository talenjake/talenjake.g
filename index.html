Investment View

Disciplined basis, moderate growth, conservative exit.

Investment Thesis

  • The subject is listed at a 6.08% cap rate, above the Q2 2026 Seattle multifamily market average used in the model.
  • The $187,500 per-unit asking basis is below two recent small-property Seattle transactions reviewed in the comp set.
  • Recent roof and electrical work reduce some near-term capital uncertainty.
  • The base case does not rely on a large immediate rent mark-to-market.

Key Risks

  • Initial levered cash yield is modest at the assumed debt cost.
  • Eight units create tenant concentration and limited operating scale.
  • Citywide asking rents are market context, not property-level Lake City rent comps.
  • Returns are sensitive to exit cap-rate expansion and operating performance.

Methodology

Public data where available. Assumptions where necessary.

Public Listing Economics

The model begins with the public asking price, unit count, occupancy, cap rate, GRM, and gross square footage from the subject listing.

Derived Operating Data

Because the public listing does not include the full rent roll or operating statement, gross potential rent and expenses are derived from the public GRM, occupancy, and cap rate.

Analyst Assumptions

Debt terms, acquisition costs, growth rates, capital reserves, selling costs, hold period, and exit cap are clearly labeled assumptions for educational underwriting.
